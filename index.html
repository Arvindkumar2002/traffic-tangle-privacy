Privacy Policy — Traffic Tangle

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy applies to the Android application Traffic Tangle: Parking Puzzle (package com.traffictangle.game), published by GroScore. This policy explains what the app collects and why. Please read it alongside the Play Store listing's Data safety section.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game needs no account, no sign-in and no personal details. Your progress — the level you have reached, your stars, your daily streak — is stored only on your own device and is never uploaded to us. Removing the app removes it.

Advertising

The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level)
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • interactions with the ads shown, for fraud prevention and measurement

This data is collected by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you. Google's handling of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device, and switch off ad personalisation there.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us and we will act on it.

Permissions

The app requests internet access and the advertising ID solely to load advertisements. The game is fully playable offline; without a connection, ads simply do not appear. No camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location are accessed.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising provider described above. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on request; advertising data is retained by Google under its own policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As the data above is held by Google rather than by us, those requests are best directed to Google, but we will help where we can.
